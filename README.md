# Eat Cake U Radio

**The radio stays on.**

Eat Cake U Radio is the house station: 39 pieces in rotation — songs, speeches, and poetry — made by me and my people, playing free around the clock. No account. No ads in your ear. The clients eat first.

**Listen:** https://radio.nema-anonymous.workers.dev
